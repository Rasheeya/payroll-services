Payroll Glossary

Common terms explained.

ACH Transfer
Automated Clearing House is an electronic network for financial transactions in the US. ACH processes large volumes of credit and debit transactions in batches.
Garnishment
A legal procedure in which a person's earnings are required by court order to be withheld by an employer for the payment of a debt such as child support.
Gross Pay vs Net Pay
Gross pay is what employees earn before taxes, benefits and other payroll deductions are withheld. Net pay is the amount they take home after all deductions.
