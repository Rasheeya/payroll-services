Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to our most common inquiries.

How long does onboarding typically take?
For companies under 50 employees, we can get you fully onboarded in under 48 hours. Enterprise clients usually take 1-2 weeks depending on data migration complexity.
Do you handle international payroll?
Yes, STACKLY currently supports localized payroll compliance and tax filing in over 45 countries, with more being added every quarter.
Can I integrate STACKLY with my existing accounting software?
Absolutely. We have native, two-way sync integrations with QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite, and Sage. We also offer a robust Open API for custom integrations.
Is my data secure?
Security is our top priority. STACKLY is SOC 2 Type II compliant, uses bank-level 256-bit encryption, and requires MFA for all administrative access.
